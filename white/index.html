What the program covers

The material is designed to improve health literacy around neuropathy. It does not diagnose a condition and does not replace a clinician, medication, physical examination, laboratory testing, or individualized medical care.

Neuropathy Basics

Learn commonly used terms related to peripheral neuropathy, peripheral nerves, sensation, and nerve function.

Symptom Awareness

Learn how clinicians may discuss sensations such as tingling, numbness, burning, sensitivity, or discomfort without using the guide to self-diagnose.

Conversation Checklist

Organize questions, timelines, medications, daily observations, and other information that may be useful during a medical appointment.

Daily Wellness Topics
